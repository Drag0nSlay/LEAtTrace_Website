What the suite does, end to end

From a single address to a mapped network of counterparties, built for the pace of an active investigation.

EX‑01
Wallet profiling
Confirmed balance, live UTXOs, first/last activity, and full transaction history for any BTC or EVM‑compatible address, pulled straight from chain data.
EX‑02
Fund flow visualization
Interactive graphs trace inflows and outflows across hops, surfacing counterparty clusters and the shortest path between two wallets.
EX‑03
AI forensic intelligence
Pattern models flag mixing behavior, layering, and structuring — narrowing thousands of transactions down to the ones worth a closer look.
EX‑04
Case management
Attach evidence, tag entities, and keep a running record inside one workspace — built to hold up as part of a case file.
EX‑05
Watchlists & alerts
Add addresses to a standing watchlist and get notified the moment a monitored wallet moves.
